# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Dynamic Agent Topology welcomes technical disagreement, critical review, and evidence that challenges existing assumptions. Participation must remain respectful, specific, and focused on the work.

Expected behavior includes:

- critique claims, designs, experiments, and code without attacking people;
- distinguish evidence from interpretation and preference;
- disclose relevant uncertainty and conflicts of interest;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries;
- give contributors credit for their work and ideas.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, or knowingly introducing malicious content.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces managed by the maintainers.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainer through a private channel rather than escalating the incident publicly. Reports will be reviewed in context and may result in content removal, warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers are expected to apply these standards consistently, including to their own participation.
